# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. We focus on technical accuracy, honest security engineering, and constructive collaboration.

## Standards

- Cite primary sources (php.net, nodejs.org, GHSA, distro security trackers) when discussing advisories.
- Do not post or link to unverified third-party exploit or PoC repositories.
- Respect maintainers and users operating legacy infrastructure under real-world constraints.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers via `security@webwhizy.com`.
